Who's behind it

One machine, a lot of ideas.

Scorchmark is a one-person shop running a precision laser out of the garage — turning wood, slate, metal and glass into things people actually keep. Every order is cut, checked, and shipped by hand.

How fast is turnaround?

Quotes within ~48 hours. Most orders ship 5–10 days after you approve the proof, depending on size.

Do you ship or is it local pickup?

Both — free local pickup, or flat-rate shipping anywhere in the US.

What files do you need for a logo?

A PNG or SVG works great. No file? Send whatever you've got and we'll clean it up.

What's the minimum order?

One. Whether it's a single gift or 500 corporate pieces, same care.
